Privacy Policy

Family Gift Tracker (shown on your home screen as “Wishlist”) · Effective November 12, 2026

Family Gift Tracker is a private wishlist app for a family. It doesn't collect any data about you. There are no accounts to create, no servers of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Where your data is kept

  • Your lists, items, pictures and notes are stored on your device and in your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit.
  • Lists you choose to share are shared, through iCloud, only with the people in your family circle, who join by invitation. Lists set to “Only me” stay in your own iCloud and are never shared.
  • When someone reserves a gift for you, that reservation is kept where you can't see it, so surprises stay surprises. Private notes on a reservation are visible only to the person who wrote them.
  • We, the developer, have no access to any of this. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What the app contacts

  • iCloud, to sync and share your lists.
  • The website of a link you add, to fetch the product's title, picture, description and price. Only that site is contacted, and only when you add the link.
  • Google Shopping, only if you tap “Find elsewhere”. That opens a search for the item's title in your browser.

The app includes no third-party code, so nothing else receives any information from it.

Removing your data

  • Remove an item and it disappears for everyone; it's kept in Recently removed for 30 days, then deleted from iCloud for good. Deleting a list removes it for everyone.
  • Leave the family circle in the app's Settings, and your lists disappear for the others, except lists you share with someone else.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from your device. To remove what's in your iCloud, go to Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage on your iPhone or iPad.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
