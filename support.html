Family Gift Tracker (shown on your home screen as “Wishlist”)

Family Gift Tracker is a private wishlist app for one family. Everyone keeps their own lists, sees each other's, and quietly marks what they're buying without the person who wants it finding out.

Getting started

  • Joining your family: open the invite link you were sent in Messages, install the app, and accept. Pick your name and color, and your empty My List is ready.
  • Adding an item: paste a product link into My Lists, or tap Share in Safari or a store app and choose Wishlist.
  • Giving: open someone's lists from Family, choose an item and tap Reserve. After you've bought it, tap Mark purchased. The person it's for never sees this.

Common questions

  • The app asks me to sign in to iCloud. It uses iCloud to keep and share your lists. Sign in under Settings › your name, then open the app again.
  • I don't see someone's new items. Close the app and open it again; it syncs whenever it opens.
  • A link didn't show a picture or price. Some stores block previews. The item is still saved with its link, and you can type the details or choose a photo yourself.
  • Can the person a gift is for see that I reserved it? No. Reservations are stored where the people an item is for can't read them, on any screen or device.
